<objective>
Close the D-31 sequence and open the observation window.

Plan 33-12 confirmed a real Sleep-Focus-off trigger produces a briefing. That was the hard
prerequisite. Now — and only now — the polling path comes out: `handle_tick`'s
`pending → sync_detected → sent` state machine, the `(10, 15)` cutoff, the retry counter,
the `/cron/morning-briefing-tick` route, the Cloud Scheduler job, and the heartbeat
staleness key that pointed at it. Retiring any of that before the trigger was live would
have produced a silent-morning gap; retiring it after leaves a `_CRON_MAX_STALENESS_HOURS`
key pointed at a dead ledger entry, which eventually fires a false critical.

Then `OCCASION_CASCADE=true` opens the 3-4 day dual-run window for the nightly and weekly
(D-30: morning has no legacy path left to compare against, and ships cascade-only). D-29 is
explicit about how the window is judged: Amit's judgment from the daily messages. The
messages are either better than the templates or they aren't; no metric answers that, and
there is no checklist gate. D-34 is the correction loop — if the morning stops mentioning
fuel, he says so, and under Phase 31 that becomes a standing directive that fixes it
permanently. No old-vs-new output diffing.

**Explicitly out of scope:** deleting the legacy composers, `prompts/nightly_review.md` /
`morning_briefing.md` / `weekly_training_review.md`, and the `OCCASION_CASCADE` flag itself.
That is Phase 35, after this window (33-CONTEXT.md § Deferred Ideas, and STATE.md's v6.0
sequencing constraint: "flag introduction strictly before legacy composer deletion — never
collapsed into one change").

Purpose: OCC-06's rollout completion.
Output: the polling path deleted, the cron retired, the flag on, and a judged window.
</objective>
