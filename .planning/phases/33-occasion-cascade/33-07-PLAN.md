<objective>
Give the morning briefing a push-triggered cascade entry point.

This is the phase's largest override. D-08/D-09 replace the Garmin wake-up anchor with an
iOS Sleep-Focus-*off* trigger — the exact mirror of the existing Sleep-Focus-*on* nightly
automation. Push beats poll: no detection latency, no 06:00 floor, no polling cost. Along
with it go the 10:15 cutoff, the `pending → sync_detected → sent` state machine and the
retry counter. Sleep data becomes briefing *content*, not a trigger.

Two sequencing constraints shape this plan. First, D-31: `/trigger/morning` ships dark and
`morning-briefing-tick` keeps running until Amit's Shortcut is confirmed firing — so
`handle_tick` and the legacy `run_morning_briefing` stay alive and untouched here, and are
deleted in plan 33-12 after the human confirmation checkpoint. Second, D-30: morning ships
cascade-only, so the new entry point has **no** `OCCASION_CASCADE` branch.

Purpose: OCC-02. The morning is the surface Amit sees first every day, so it gets the new
architecture without an A/B — misbehaviour surfaces within one day.
Output: `run_morning_briefing_triggered`, the D-05/D-06 write-timing inversion, the D-15
widened window, the D-20 Sunday deferral signal, and the D-14 manual path.
</objective>
