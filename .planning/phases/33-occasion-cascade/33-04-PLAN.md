<objective>
Generalize the tick's 3-layer cascade so the three occasions can run through it. This is
the structural heart of the phase: after this plan, `nightly` / `morning` /
`weekly_review` are just parameters.

The move (33-RESEARCH § Pattern 1, 33-PATTERNS § cascade orchestration): keep each
occasion's own specialised Layer-0 gather where it lives, and route its output through
the **same** Layer-1 triage (`TickBrain.think` + `autonomous_triage.md`) and Layer-2
compose (`_run_smart_loop` + `autonomous.md`) the tick already uses. Two gates that exist
purely for tick efficiency — the empty-signals gate and the Layer-0.5 change-detection
gate — are bypassed when an occasion is set, because an occasion fires on a schedule or a
push trigger, not on signal novelty.

Purpose: without this, plans 33-06/07/08 have nothing to call, and D-36's "one place to
change behavior" collapses into three parallel pipelines.
Output: `run_occasion_cascade()` plus a shared `_run_cascade()` body in
`core/autonomous.py`, with `run_autonomous_tick` refactored onto the same body.
</objective>
