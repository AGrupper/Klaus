<objective>
Write the prompt half of this phase. D-36 is the organising rule: cross-cutting behaviour
goes in the two **shared** cascade prompts so the `*/20` tick inherits every improvement,
and each occasion gets only a few lines of its own identity (D-35). D-33 is the
discipline: knowledge lives in the gathered data and the callable tools, not in an
enumerated checklist — the legacy composers' fuel plans, "Week N of 16" framings and
macro-accountability sections are deliberately NOT transcribed into these prompts.

The hard constraint is the Groq per-request admission ceiling. `prompts/autonomous_triage.md`
is rendered as the system prompt on every one of the ~43 daily tick calls, and
`tests/test_token_budget.py` currently measures the maximal rendered triage prompt +
`TICK_BRAIN_MAX_TOKENS` at 7,146 against a 7,200 target — a 54-token margin. This plan
carries an explicit, pre-authorised fallback for that constraint (Task 1) so the executor
never has to choose between shipping D-02 and passing the guard.

Purpose: Layer 1 and Layer 2 cannot implement judgment they were never told about.
Output: two edited shared prompts, three new occasion prompts, prompt-shape tests.
</objective>
